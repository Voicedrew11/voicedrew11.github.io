## Hosting
When picking a host, you are choosing between convenience and independence/control; A VPS offers the most control, but it also requires the most technical knowledge and has recurring upkeep costs. On the other side of the spectrum, 

- VPS
- GitHub/GitLab pages
- Neocities/Nekoweb
- Bearblog

**I do not recommend Neocities.**




## SSG

If you are new to having a website, you may feel tempted to use a static site generator. This was indeed how my website started out. I downloaded HUGO and a theme for it, and started building.
